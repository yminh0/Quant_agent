## Acceptance-axis coverage

| Axis | Covered | Evidence |

### The `/readiness` axis is covered only for the in-memory store

`_release_readiness` in `ai/ai_graph/api.py` computes `durable_store_ready` from a
conjunction that includes `runtime.dsn_configured`, so a missing DSN does force
`status: unavailable`. The passing test, however, drives that state by constructing an
`InMemoryAnalysisJobStore`, not by clearing the DSN. The DSN term is established by
reading the code, not by a test that varies it. A test that sets `AI_JOB_STORE=persistent`
with no DSN and asserts `503` would close the axis on its own terms.

### The recommendation axis is contradicted at this SHA

`tests/test_graph_e2e.py::test_rsi_strategy_runs_ready_e2e_without_external_keys` passes,
and it asserts the opposite of this axis:

- `envelope.status == "ready"`
- `envelope.user_payload.report` is present, with both `web_projection` and
  `email_projection`
- `internal.validation["pipeline_data_source"]["source"] == "fixture"`

It runs with no DSN and no provider credentials. `load_pipeline_data_from_env` falls back
to a fixture bundle — `tests/test_db_data_source.py::test_pipeline_data_source_without_dsn_uses_explicit_nonproduction_fixture`
pins that fallback, asserting `metadata["source"] == "fixture"` and
`metadata["production_eligible"] is False`.

So without a DSN the system does not withhold a recommendation. It produces one from
fixture data and **labels** it non-production. Labelling is a weaker guarantee than the
axis states.

### The positive-metric axis has no test either way

Nothing in the suite asserts that a no-DSN run yields zero positive quantitative metrics.
Since the fixture path produces a full report, the presumption from the two facts above is
that positive metrics are reachable without a DSN, but this bundle does not demonstrate
that directly and does not claim it.

## What this bundle does and does not establish

It establishes that the fixed command exits `0` at this SHA and that the release
readiness endpoint fails closed for a non-durable job store. It does not establish the
row's stated acceptance. Two of three axes are unmet as written: one is contradicted by a
passing test, one is untested.

The gap is the same one `FT-FIX-08` exists to close — restricting fixture mode to a
development profile. Until that lands, `FT-RLS-01` cannot be closed on its current wording
without either narrowing the axes to the `/readiness` endpoint alone or suppressing the
fixture fallback.
