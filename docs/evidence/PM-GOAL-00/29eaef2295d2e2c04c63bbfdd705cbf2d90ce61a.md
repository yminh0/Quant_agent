failing. The rejection behaviour the axis describes is exercised only against synthetic
boards constructed inside `scripts/check-production-plan.test.mjs` — tests 2, 4, and 5
above cover a missing recurrence count, a non-existent or all-zero snapshot SHA, and a
malformed board respectively.

repository's own document no longer reproduces a FAIL, and nothing in this bundle
restores that. Closing the row on its literal wording requires either a recorded FAIL
output from the revision that predates the field fill, or a rewritten axis that names
the negative unit tests as the intended evidence.
