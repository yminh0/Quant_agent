## What "state 보존" means in the implementation

The row's wording could be read as "a running job is still running after a restart". That
is not what the code does, and the difference matters for reading the tests.

A restart preserves the job **record**, and reconciles its **status**. A job that a dead
process left `RUNNING` or `QUEUED` is settled to `FAILED` at startup, before the app
serves, carrying `INTERRUPTED_BY_RESTART_REASON` in `fallback_reasons` and a `FAILED`
envelope so a polling client sees the outcome rather than a job stuck running forever.
The alternative — leaving the row `RUNNING` — is the defect this design removes.

## Not covered

- **No test restarts an actual process.** `test_startup_sweeps_the_store_before_serving`
  simulates a restart by stamping a foreign `owner_incarnation` on the row and then
  entering the app's lifespan against an `InMemoryAnalysisJobStore`. Durability is covered
  separately as a serialization round-trip. Neither exercises a real process boundary with
  a real PostgreSQL store, so "restart" is modelled, not performed.
- The deployed probes read configuration state. They do not demonstrate that a restart on
  the server produced the reconciliation this row requires; that needs a restart performed
  there with the store read before and after.
